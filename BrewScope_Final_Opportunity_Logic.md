# BrewScope — Final Opportunity Logic

## Problem definition
India RTD coffee is an occasional purchase because fresh coffee is preferred. The project therefore searches for a specific high-need occasion rather than a generic category expansion.

## Evidence chain
1. Competitive shelf: mass RTD products compete on low absolute price; Sleepy Owl already plays around ₹99/230 ml; premium specialty players sit higher.
2. Functional scan: protein is already occupied by visible competitors; generic focus is also not empty because NERV owns a direct focus RTD.
3. Consumer input: convenience + caffeine are strongest triggers; late-night study/work is the clearest occasion; taste and price are key barriers; ₹79 is the maximum acceptable target price.
4. Brand fit: Sleepy Owl is already active in RTD coffee and has broader coffee credentials.
5. Synthesis: the most plausible next opportunity is a simple coffee-first RTD designed around the late-night study/work moment, within an accessible price corridor.

## Final working concept
Sleepy Owl Study / Work Focus Latte
- 200 ml
- ₹59–79 target corridor
- ₹79 ceiling
- measured moderate caffeine
- controlled/low sugar
- coffee-first milk format
- quick-commerce first
- communication anchored on study/work occasion

## Decision standard
This is a “proceed to concept development” recommendation, not a launch approval. The unresolved questions are unit economics, taste/sensory acceptance, size of the occasion, and claim/compliance feasibility.
